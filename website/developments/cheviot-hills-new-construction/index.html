The neighborhood

A classic Westside neighborhood

Cheviot Hills is known for quiet, winding streets and some of the best park space on the Westside.

Century City, Culver City and Westwood are minutes away, and the Expo Line is convenient to Santa Monica and Downtown.

Good to Know

Before you tour, answered.

When will the home be finished?

Anticipated completion is Q4 2026, within the next one to three months, per the builder.

How big is the home?

About 4,403 square feet with 5 bedrooms and 5.5 bathrooms, on an approximately 7,200 square foot lot. Figures are per builder plans.

What is the ADU like?

A separate 1-bedroom, 1-bath unit with its own living and dining area. It works as guest quarters, a home office or a separate suite.

Does it have a pool?

Yes, a pool and spa in a private backyard.

How do I confirm which school a home is assigned to?

Attendance boundaries don't follow zip codes, so two homes a few blocks apart can be assigned to different schools. We confirm the assigned schools for the specific home before you write an offer.
