When will these homes be finished?

Where are the homes?

One in Mar Vista (90066), one in Cheviot Hills (90064) and two in Carlson Park, Culver City (90232). Exact locations are shared on request.

Do they all have an ADU or guest suite?

Yes. Mar Vista has a ground-floor guest suite, and the other three each have an ADU, from 1 bedroom up to 2 bedrooms and 2 baths.

How can I see them?

How do I confirm which school a home is assigned to?

Attendance boundaries don't follow zip codes, so two homes a few blocks apart can be assigned to different schools. We confirm the assigned schools for the specific home before you write an offer.
