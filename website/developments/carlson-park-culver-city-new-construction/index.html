Good to Know

Before you tour, answered.

When will the homes be finished?

Both have anticipated completion in Q4 2026, within the next one to three months, per the builder.

What's the difference between the two homes?

Home one is about 4,339 SF with a 1-bedroom ADU and a 2-car garage. Home two is about 3,930 SF with a larger 2-bedroom, 2-bath ADU and tuck-under parking. Both have 5 bedrooms and 5.5 baths.

What school district are these homes in?

Carlson Park is in Culver City, within the Culver City Unified School District. Buyers should verify school assignment for the specific home; we help confirm it before you write an offer.

Can the ADU be rented?

ADUs may generally be rented, subject to Culver City rules. We'll walk you through the rules and realistic rents.
