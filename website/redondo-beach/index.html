Beach Cities · South Bay

Redondo Beach
real estate agent.

The largest and most varied Beach City — a working harbor, Riviera Village, and a North Redondo townhome market that gets families into Beach Cities schools for less.

90277 · 90278

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · By Michael Abraham, licensed architect & REALTOR®, DRE# 02242095

Redondo Beach is the value play of the Beach Cities. The median sale price was about $1.57M in the three months to August 2026 (Redfin), roughly $1.9M below Manhattan Beach, with homes selling in a median 44 days at 99.7% of list.

It is really two markets. South Redondo (90277) is coastal — the pier, King Harbor and Riviera Village. North Redondo (90278) is inland and family-driven, with newer townhomes and single-family homes on regular lots. The right answer depends on whether you are buying the beach or buying the school district.

At a glance

Redondo Beach — five things to know.

01

North vs South

The 90277/90278 split is the first question. South is the coast and Riviera Village; North is inland, quieter and larger for the money.

02

King Harbor and the pier

Redondo is the only Beach City with a working marina. Harbor-adjacent condos form their own sub-market with HOA and building-age questions of their own.

03

Riviera Village

The walkable shopping and dining district at the south end of the city, next to the Palos Verdes hills, anchors the highest-priced streets in South Redondo.

04

The townhome belt

Much of North Redondo has been rebuilt as two-on-a-lot townhomes. Quality varies a lot by builder, which is where an architect’s read pays for itself.

The architect's checklist

What we check before you buy in Redondo Beach

Builder and year
For newer townhomes: who built it, when, and whether there is a construction-defect history in the complex or with the builder.
Shared walls
Recorded agreements for shared walls, roofs and driveways on two-on-a-lot properties.
Permit history
Additions and conversions against the city record, especially on older South Redondo homes.
HOA health
For harbor-area condos: reserve study, special assessments and insurance coverage.
Coastal zone
Whether the parcel sits in the coastal zone and what that means for a future remodel.

Neighbors

Where it sits in the South Bay.

North across the city line is Hermosa Beach — smaller lots, walk streets and higher prices per square foot. Beyond that is Manhattan Beach. For how all four compare side by side, see the Beach Cities guide; for the investment math on a Redondo duplex, send it to our Cash & Flow desk.

Redondo Beach FAQ

Redondo Beach, explained.

How much does a home cost in Redondo Beach?

Redfin put the Redondo Beach median sale price at about $1.57M for the three months to August 2026, up 1.8% year over year, at roughly $889 per square foot. It is the most accessible of the Beach Cities on price.

What is the difference between North and South Redondo?

South Redondo (ZIP 90277) runs along the water from the pier to Riviera Village and the Palos Verdes edge, and prices higher. North Redondo (ZIP 90278) is inland, with more single-family homes and newer townhomes, larger floor plans for the money, and quicker freeway access.

What school district serves Redondo Beach?

Redondo Beach Unified School District, including Redondo Union High School. Confirm the assigned elementary and middle school for the specific address with the district.

Does Redondo Beach have rent control?

There is no city rent-control ordinance. Qualifying rentals fall under California's statewide Tenant Protection Act (AB 1482). Confirm the status of a specific property with an attorney.

Is Redondo Beach a good place to buy a townhome or duplex?

It is one of the better places in the South Bay for it. North Redondo in particular has a deep stock of newer side-by-side townhomes built on former single-family lots, which gives buyers Beach Cities schools at a lower entry price. Check the HOA or shared-wall agreement and the construction-defect history.
