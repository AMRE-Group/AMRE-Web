The plan

Launch into January, sold by March

The house is empty by Nov 30, prepped and staged the first week of December, and photographed Dec 11. Here is how we take it to market from there.

Mon, Dec 14

Coming Soon

Your home shows as Coming Soon on the MLS, Compass.com, Redfin and international sites. Buyers with a matching Compass search are notified, and days on market stay at zero.

Thu, Jan 7

Go live

Full launch in the first full week of January, when serious buyers are back. Open house Sunday, Jan 10, then the broker open on Tuesday, Jan 12.

Around Mar 8

Sold

We aim for offers in the first two to four weeks, then about 30 days of escrow. That puts closing around Mar 8, 60 days after launch.

Why not go live in December? A listing that launches Dec 17 sits through the two slowest weeks of the year and collects days on market. Coming Soon builds a buyer list over the holidays, and the launch on Jan 7 keeps the listing fresh.
