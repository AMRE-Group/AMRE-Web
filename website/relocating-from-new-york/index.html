What Changes

Six things New Yorkers relearn in the first month.

The neighborhood match gets you close. These adjustments decide whether it actually feels right day to day.

Blocks from the train

Minutes from work

Your radius is set by the drive, not the walk. Pick the side of town your job is on first, then pick the neighborhood.

One city, one weather

Microclimates

The coast runs cool with a May–June marine layer. A few miles inland can be 10–20°F warmer on the same summer afternoon.

The co-op board interview

An HOA, not a board

Most Westside purchases are single-family homes or condos. California buyers usually encounter an HOA review rather than a New York–style co-op board interview.

Closing attorneys

Escrow does the closing

California closings run through a neutral escrow company. Around 30 days from accepted offer is typical with a loan.

A storage unit in Queens

Space becomes a line item

Garage, yard, guest room, even a backyard ADU for rental income. Budget for the life you'll spread into.

The unlimited MetroCard

Transit, selectively

The Metro E Line runs Santa Monica to Downtown. Living near a station is a real perk; living without a car is rare.

New York FAQ

Questions New Yorkers ask us.

Where in LA feels most like Manhattan?

There's no single match. West Hollywood offers density and nightlife; Santa Monica offers walkable streets, restaurants and the coast; Downtown LA offers the most urban setting. Your favorite part of Manhattan matters more than the borough name.

Can I live in Los Angeles without a car?

Yes, if you choose your home around a specific commute and daily routine. Santa Monica, Culver City and parts of Downtown are worth exploring, especially near the Metro E Line, which connects Santa Monica and Downtown LA. Test the actual trip from a prospective home before deciding.

Should I rent before buying?

If you haven't spent time in LA, a short lease can help you learn your commute and preferred blocks. If you already know the area and plan to stay, start the purchase search before your move.

Can you tour homes before I fly out?

Yes. We can preview homes, share video walkthroughs, and flag layout, condition and location issues, so you can use your scouting trip on the strongest options.
