1. Information We Collect

Information you give us

When you submit a contact form, request a home valuation, download a market report, register for an open house, or otherwise reach out, we collect the information you provide — typically your first and last name, email address, phone number, property address, and any notes about your goals, timeline, or search criteria. We also collect your communication preferences and consents, including whether you have opted in to receive texts or emails.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, we and our Service Providers automatically log technical data when you visit: device and browser type, operating system, screen resolution, IP address, approximate location (city/state level, derived from IP), referring URL, pages and screens viewed, time on page, navigation paths, access times, and whether you opened our emails or clicked links in them. We collect this using cookies, local storage, and web beacons (pixel tags).

Information from third parties

Social platforms: If you interact with our pages on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, or YouTube, those platforms' own privacy policies govern their collection, and they may pass limited information to us.

Public and commercial records: We may supplement our records with information from property and public records databases, the MLS, and commercially available data providers so we can serve you more relevant information.

Communication and CRM data

If you email, text, or call us, we may retain the content, metadata, timestamps, delivery status, and attachments of those communications in our CRM, along with notes about our conversations. Our CRM may synchronize with our email accounts so that correspondence with you is kept in one place. This is used to serve you and keep accurate records — not to build a profile for resale.

2. How We Use Your Information

To provide and improve the Services: respond to your inquiries, prepare valuations and reports, show you properties, operate and improve the Site, and send administrative or security messages.

To manage our relationship with you: maintain records of our communications, track your stated preferences, and provide personalized service.

To follow up: if you have asked us to, we may contact you by phone, email, or SMS about your inquiry. You can opt out at any time (see Section 6).

To send marketing you opted into: new listing alerts, price changes, open house invitations, market updates, and our newsletter. Other than as described in Section 4, your information is never shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

To comply with law and protect people: respond to lawful requests and legal process; investigate and deter fraud, abuse, or unauthorized activity; and establish or defend legal claims.

To create anonymous and aggregated data: we may de-identify information and use or share the resulting aggregate data (which cannot identify you) for analytics and research.

3. Use of Artificial Intelligence

We use AI and machine-learning tools to help produce content and manage communications. AI may assist in drafting property descriptions, blog posts, market commentary, newsletters, social posts, and replies to inquiries. A human reviews material communications before they go out, though some routine automated responses may be sent without individual review.

AI-generated content can contain errors or omissions. Nothing on this Site — AI-assisted or not — should be relied on as the sole basis for a real estate, financial, legal, or tax decision without independent verification. You may ask us whether AI was used to generate specific content, request human review of a decision made with AI assistance, or opt out of AI-powered features where technically feasible; contact us at Section 13.

4. How We Share Your Information (and What We Never Do)

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not disclose your phone number, email address, or SMS consent to any third party for that party's marketing or promotional purposes, and we do not sell personal information as defined under applicable law. We are not in the business of selling leads.

We share your information only in these limited circumstances:

Our own team: the agents, employees, and contractors of AMRE Real Estate Group, and where a transaction requires it, our brokerage, Compass.

Service Providers acting on our behalf: vendors that make our business run — website hosting, CRM and email delivery, e-signature, transaction coordination, analytics, and AI tooling. They may only use your information to perform services for us.

Transaction participants, when you ask us to represent you: in an active transaction, information necessary to complete it may be shared with the other side's agent, escrow, title, lenders, inspectors, and similar parties.

Professional advisors: lawyers, accountants, auditors, and insurers, where needed for the services they provide us.

Legal compliance, fraud prevention, and safety: where we believe disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect rights, property, or safety.

Business transfers: if our business or its assets are merged, acquired, reorganized, or sold, your information may transfer as part of that transaction. We will make reasonable efforts to require the recipient to honor this Policy.

5. Cookies, Advertising, and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to operate the Site, remember preferences, measure traffic, and understand which content is useful.

Advertising platforms. We advertise our own services on platforms including Google and Meta (Facebook/Instagram). To do that, those platforms may collect device identifiers and browsing activity through cookies and pixels on our Site, and we may use that data to measure campaign performance, attribute conversions, and build lookalike audiences. This is advertising for AMRE — it is not the sale of your contact information, and no third party receives your phone number or SMS consent for its own marketing.

Interest-based advertising. You may see our ads on other sites based on your prior visits here. See Section 6 to opt out.

6. Your Choices and Opt-Outs

Text messages

Reply STOP to any text from us and you will be unsubscribed immediately. Reply HELP for assistance.

Email

Click the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any marketing email, or email us and we will remove you.

Phone calls

Tell us on any call, or contact us in writing, and we will place you on our internal do-not-call list.

Cookies and interest-based ads

Most browsers let you block or delete cookies — see allaboutcookies.org. You can also limit advertising-ID use in your mobile settings, use privacy-focused browsers or plug-ins, and opt out through Google Ads Settings, Meta ad preferences, the Digital Advertising Alliance, and the Network Advertising Initiative. These opt-outs are per-browser and per-device.

Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not currently respond to browser "Do Not Track" signals, which lack a common standard. We do honor Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals as valid opt-out requests where applicable law requires.

Access, correction, and deletion

You may ask us what personal information we hold about you, request a copy, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Contact us using Section 13 and we will respond as described in Section 12.

7. Data Retention

When information is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymize it.

12. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, the California Consumer Privacy Act as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act ("CCPA") gives you the rights described below.

Categories we collect

In the preceding 12 months we may have collected: identifiers (name, email, phone, postal address, IP address); commercial information (property preferences, transaction history); internet or network activity (browsing and interaction data); approximate geolocation derived from IP address; professional or employment information, if you provide it; and inferences drawn from the above, such as your likely price range or neighborhood interest.

Selling and sharing

We do not sell personal information within the meaning of the CCPA. We may "share" limited online identifiers (such as cookie IDs and browsing activity) with advertising partners for cross-context behavioral advertising as described in Section 5; you have the right to opt out of that sharing, and can do so by contacting us or by sending a Global Privacy Control signal. We do not sell or share the personal information of consumers we know to be under 16.

Your rights

You may request to know the categories and specific pieces of personal information we have collected, the sources, the purposes, and the categories of recipients; to correct inaccurate information; to delete information we hold; to opt out of sharing for cross-context behavioral advertising; and to limit our use of sensitive personal information (we do not collect sensitive personal information for the Site beyond any account credentials, which are used only to secure your account). You have the right not to be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

How to submit a request

Email or call us using Section 13. We may ask for information to verify your identity, used only for verification. We acknowledge requests within ten (10) business days and respond within 45 calendar days; if we need up to 90 days, we will tell you why in writing. Disclosures cover the 12 months preceding your request. There is no fee unless a request is excessive, repetitive, or manifestly unfounded, in which case we will tell you first. An authorized agent may submit a request on your behalf with your written permission or a valid power of attorney.
