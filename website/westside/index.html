Location, Location, Location

The right house on the wrong block will always underperform.

Every city says location matters. In Los Angeles it's the whole game — and here, "location" can change by the mile, the street, even which side of a single zip code you land on. We've built our careers on the Westside, and that knowledge never shows up in a listing photo. It shows up in the questions we ask before you ever write an offer.

One zip code, two different worlds

Nowhere is this truer than school boundaries. Across the Westside it's common for a single zip code to split quietly into more than one school attendance area: two homes a few blocks apart, same freeway exit, same mail carrier, entirely different assigned schools.

Common Questions

The Westside, explained.

What counts as the Westside of Los Angeles?+

There's no official boundary. In practice the Westside runs west of La Cienega or La Brea to the ocean, and roughly from the Santa Monica Mountains down to LAX. It typically includes Santa Monica, Venice, Brentwood, Pacific Palisades, Mar Vista, Culver City, Playa Vista, Westwood, Beverly Hills and Bel Air — with Malibu to the north and West Adams on the eastern edge.

Why do two homes on the same street get different schools?+

Because school attendance areas are drawn independently of zip codes. A single Westside zip code often splits into more than one attendance area, and those boundaries change over time. We verify the assigned school for the specific address rather than trusting a listing or a portal.

Which Westside neighborhood is right for me?+

It comes down to how you actually spend your week — commute, walkability, whether you want a yard or a boardwalk, and whether the purchase also has to work as an investment. Beach-adjacent Santa Monica and Venice trade space for proximity; Brentwood and Pacific Palisades trade proximity for space and quiet; Culver City and Mar Vista sit at the center of the job corridors; West Adams offers historic architecture at a different entry point. That's the first conversation we have.

How do I see off-market Westside homes?+

Through an agent inside the network. Compass Private Exclusives are shared agent-to-agent before a home is publicly listed, and a real share of Westside deals get made in conversation before photos are even taken.
