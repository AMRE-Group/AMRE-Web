Marina del Rey
real estate.

A County-governed harbor of attached homes, ground leases and coastal-zone rules — where the ownership structure matters as much as the view.

The Peninsula · Silver Strand · The Mole Roads

Last updated: September 8, 2026

Marina del Rey is a mostly attached-housing market inside unincorporated Los Angeles County, built around a County-owned harbor. Condominiums and apartments line the mole roads, single-family and townhome product sits on the Marina Peninsula and Silver Strand, and value turns on three documents: the ground lease, the HOA package and the coastal-zone status of the parcel.

That is the short version, and it is the part most buyers get wrong. Marina del Rey looks like Venice with better parking. It behaves like a different market, because the land underneath a large share of the residential buildings is not owned the way land is owned two blocks north. Read the title report before you fall in love with the balcony.

AMRE represents buyers and sellers across the harbor — Via Marina and the mole roads, the walk streets of the Marina Peninsula, Silver Strand, and the Admiralty Way corridor toward Lincoln Boulevard. Michael trained as an architect, which is why our first pass on a Marina del Rey building is the envelope: balconies, railings, window systems and podium waterproofing, the four line items that become special assessments in salt air.

At a glance

Marina del Rey — six things to know.

01

The mole roads

Panay Way, Bora Bora Way, Mindanao Way, Marquesas Way and Via Marina hold most of the condominium and apartment inventory. Buildings vary widely in vintage, ownership structure and monthly cost. Two towers on the same street can be entirely different purchases.

02

Marina Peninsula

West of Via Marina, between the main channel and the beach. Walk streets, tight lots, and the only meaningful concentration of detached and townhome product in the harbor. Beach access on one side, boat access on the other.

03

Silver Strand

Just north of the Peninsula and closer in feel to Venice than to the harbor towers. Smaller-scale residential, strong owner-occupant demand, and the shortest walk to Washington Boulevard.

04

County, not City

Marina del Rey is unincorporated Los Angeles County. Permits, planning, code enforcement and policing run through County agencies. Neighboring Venice and Playa del Rey are inside the City of Los Angeles, with different rules on the same block face.

05

Ground leases

Much of the harbor was developed on County land under long-term ground leases. Some buildings convey a leasehold, not fee simple. The remaining term is part of what you are buying, and lenders will price it that way.

06

Coastal zone

The harbor lies inside the California Coastal Zone. Exterior work, additions and demolition can trigger coastal review on top of County permits. Renovation timelines here are entitlement timelines.

Orientation

How the harbor is laid out

Schematic only, not to scale and not a boundary survey. The Peninsula and Silver Strand sit between the ocean and the main channel; the mole roads finger east into the basins; Admiralty Way and Lincoln Boulevard form the inland edge. Venice is north, Playa del Rey south, Playa Vista inland.

Underwriting checklist

What we check before you remove a contingency in Marina del Rey

Land ownership
Fee simple or leasehold? If leasehold, the remaining ground-lease term, renewal language and who holds the reversion. This is the first call, not the last.
HOA reserves
Reserve study, deferred maintenance, litigation, insurance and every special assessment in the building's recent history.
Building envelope
Balconies, railings, window and door systems, podium and deck waterproofing. Salt air is an accelerant; these are the assessments that arrive as a surprise.
Slip rights
Whether a boat slip is deeded to the unit, assigned by the association, or rented separately from the County or a marina operator.
Coastal status
Whether the parcel or the intended scope of work requires coastal review, and what that adds to the schedule.
Jurisdiction
County versus City of Los Angeles by parcel, plus the assigned LAUSD school confirmed by address rather than by neighborhood name.

Informational only, not legal advice. Confirm jurisdiction, lease terms and permitting requirements with Los Angeles County and your own attorney.

Pricing

How pricing actually works here.

There is no single Marina del Rey price. The harbor prices by building, then by exposure, then by ownership structure — and the third of those can move a number more than the first two. A fee-simple unit with a healthy reserve account and a deeded slip is a different asset from a leasehold unit in a building three years into a balcony repair, even if the floor plans match.

Water exposure is the second lever. Channel and basin views, morning versus afternoon light, and how much boat traffic passes the balcony all show up in price and in how long a listing takes. On the Peninsula, the variable is which walk street and how far to the sand.

For current sold data, price bands and days-on-market across the Westside, start with our Westside market report and then ask us for the harbor cut. We would rather give you the current numbers directly than publish a figure that goes stale.

Neighbors

Where it sits on the Westside.

North is Venice — denser, louder, inside the City of Los Angeles, and the place to look if you want architectural single-family and can live without a slip. Inland and south is Playa Vista, master-planned and HOA-governed, the most predictable purchase on this side of the Westside and the usual alternative for buyers who like new construction and one rulebook.

East across Lincoln is Mar Vista, where the lots get deep and the renovation market is genuinely active. Buyers who start in the harbor and decide they want a yard tend to end up there.

Marina del Rey FAQ

Marina del Rey, explained.

Is Marina del Rey a good place to live?

It suits people who want water access, a short commute to the Playa Vista and El Segundo job corridors, and a low-maintenance home. Most of the housing is attached — condominiums and apartments along the mole roads — with a small band of single-family and townhome product on the Marina Peninsula and Silver Strand. If you want a yard, a large lot, or a traditional street grid, Mar Vista or Playa del Rey will fit better.

What are the main sub-areas of Marina del Rey?

Four, broadly. The mole roads that finger into the harbor (Panay Way, Bora Bora Way, Mindanao Way, Marquesas Way and Via Marina) hold most of the condominium and apartment inventory. The Marina Peninsula, west of Via Marina, is the walk-street strip between the main channel and the beach. Silver Strand sits just north of the Peninsula and reads more like Venice. Admiralty Way and the Marina City Club area anchor the eastern edge toward Lincoln Boulevard.

Is Marina del Rey part of the City of Los Angeles?

No. Marina del Rey is an unincorporated area of Los Angeles County. Planning, permits, code enforcement and law enforcement run through County agencies rather than the City of Los Angeles, which changes who you call and which rules apply. Neighboring Venice and Playa del Rey are inside the City. Confirming jurisdiction by parcel before you write an offer is not optional here.

Do Marina del Rey condos sit on leased land?

Many of the residential developments in the harbor were built on County-owned land under long-term ground leases, so some buildings convey a leasehold interest rather than fee-simple ownership. That affects financing, resale and the remaining lease term you are actually buying. Ask for the ground lease and its expiration in the first week of escrow. We check the title report and the lease term on every Marina del Rey offer.

What should I look for in the HOA documents?

Reserves, deferred maintenance, litigation, insurance, and the special-assessment history. Harbor-adjacent buildings carry salt-air exposure, so balconies, railings, window systems and podium waterproofing are the line items that turn into assessments. Also check rental restrictions and whether a boat slip is deeded, assigned, or rented separately from the unit.

Does the Coastal Commission affect remodels in Marina del Rey?

Marina del Rey lies inside the California Coastal Zone, so exterior work, additions, demolition and anything affecting public access or views can require coastal review on top of County permits. That adds time and design constraints to a renovation plan. If your purchase depends on a remodel, we price the entitlement risk before you remove the contingency.

How does Marina del Rey compare with Playa Vista?

Playa Vista is master-planned, HOA-governed and inland, with newer construction and a single set of community rules. Marina del Rey is older, water-facing, County-governed and building-by-building, so two units a block apart can carry very different ownership structures and monthly costs. Playa Vista is the more predictable purchase; Marina del Rey is the one with water frontage.

Which schools serve Marina del Rey?

Marina del Rey falls inside Los Angeles Unified School District boundaries, and assignments are made by address rather than by neighborhood name. Because the harbor sits at the edge of several attendance areas, two buildings on different mole roads can feed different schools. Confirm the assigned school directly with LAUSD for the specific address before you make school access part of your decision.
