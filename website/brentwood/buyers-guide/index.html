Location, Location, Location

The right house on the wrong block always underperforms.

In Los Angeles, location is the whole game, and in Brentwood it changes by the street. Six enclaves share one zip code, from flat estate lots in Brentwood Park to acreage up Mandeville Canyon to mid-century landmarks in Crestwood Hills. This guide is the Brentwood chapter of our Buyer's Roadmap: the neighborhood the way we'd show it to you on a first drive.

Neighborhood Spotlight

A day in Brentwood.

Here's what an ordinary Tuesday in 90049 actually looks like, so you can picture yourself in it before you tour a single home.

6:30AM

Trailhead before the traffic

Westridge-Canyonback Wilderness Park starts at the top of the hill. Ridge views to the ocean and back to the city, and you're home before the first school bell.

8:00AM

The San Vicente median

The grass median under the coral trees runs from Brentwood toward Ocean Avenue. It's the neighborhood's unofficial running track, dog walk and morning catch-up.

9:30AM

Coffee at the Country Mart

The red barn on 26th Street has anchored Brentwood since the 1940s. Coffee, the courtyard, a few errands, and a familiar face or two.

11:00AM

Art on the hill

The Getty Center sits right in the neighborhood. General admission is free; reserve parking and spend an hour in the gardens.

1:00PM

Lunch on San Vicente

Brentwood's restaurant row runs along San Vicente Boulevard. Patio tables, long lunches, and usually someone you know at the next table. Our pick: Baltaire, the Brentwood steakhouse with dry-aged cuts and a lively patio bar.

3:30PM

Pickup & practice

Afternoons revolve around school runs, tennis and practice. It's also when you learn which streets back up near Sunset, a detail we check before you buy.

6:30PM

The coast at sunset

Roll down San Vicente and you're at the sand in about ten minutes. Few luxury neighborhoods put the canyon and the Pacific this close together.

8:00PM

Back up the hill

Dinner in the Village, then home. North of Sunset the streets go quiet early: dark skies, canyon air, and very little through-traffic.

Six enclaves, one zip code.

Estate living, ten minutes from the sand. Where each enclave sits, what it's like, and what it typically costs.

Schematic, not to scale. Enclave edges are approximate.

1Brentwood Park

Flat, wide estate lots on tree-canopied streets just north of San Vicente. Gated drives, deep setbacks, tennis courts and pools.

SFR $7M–$25M+

A long, rural canyon road. Acreage, privacy, horses, and the feeling of being far out of the city while staying in it.

SFR $4M–$15M

A mid-century landmark: post-war modernist homes by A. Quincy Jones and Whitney Smith, with canyon and ocean views.

Hillside streets above Sunset with view homes and a highly regarded public elementary school close by.

SFR $3.5M–$10M

South of Sunset near the 405. Smaller, walkable streets with a tight-knit, neighborly feel.

SFR $2.5M–$4.5M

Condos and townhomes near San Vicente and Barrington. Lock-and-leave living a walk from everything.

Good to Know

Buying in Brentwood, answered.

How much does a home cost in Brentwood 90049?

It depends heavily on the enclave. Typical single-family ranges from recent sales, rounded: Brentwood Park $7M–$25M+, Mandeville Canyon $4M–$15M, Kenter Canyon $3.5M–$10M, Crestwood Hills $2.5M–$5.5M, Brentwood Glen $2.5M–$4.5M, and Brentwood Village $3M–$7M. Condos and townhomes, concentrated near the Village and south of San Vicente, typically run $900K–$2.5M. We pull live comps for every home you consider.

What are the main neighborhoods inside Brentwood?

Six enclaves share the 90049 zip code: Brentwood Park, Mandeville Canyon, Crestwood Hills, Kenter Canyon, Brentwood Glen and Brentwood Village. See the map and pricing above, or the Brentwood overview for more sub-markets.

How far is Brentwood from the beach?

From central Brentwood, the Santa Monica beach is about 10 minutes down San Vicente off-peak. Century City is roughly 15 minutes, Beverly Hills 20, LAX 30 and Downtown LA 35. Rush hour can double those times.

How do I know which public school a Brentwood home is assigned to?

Attendance boundaries on the Westside don't follow zip codes: two homes a few blocks apart can be assigned to different schools. We confirm the assigned school for the specific address of every home we show you, before you write an offer.
