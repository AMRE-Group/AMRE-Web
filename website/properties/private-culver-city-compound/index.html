R2 Zoning & The Long-Term Upside

Enjoy a compound today. Hold a premier lot for tomorrow.

What truly sets this property apart is its versatile R2 zoning on a generous 5,404 sq ft lot.

Live in the main three-bedroom home while generating passive income from the studio, or lease both units for maximum return. The longer-term story is the zoning: under R2 (and local density provisions), a future owner has the option to redevelop the parcel into two large single-family homes or townhomes.
