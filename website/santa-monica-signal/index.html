Market Action Index

Thirty-five condos for sale. Buyers have room to negotiate.

The index compares the rate of sales against available inventory. At 28 — unchanged from last month — 90404 condos read as a slight buyer's advantage, and inventory has risen to 35 active listings. The index has trended lower for several weeks while list price and price per square foot have held on a plateau.

37% of listings have cut price and 31% have relisted. If supply keeps outpacing demand, pricing is the next thing to move.
