How to read this. Every figure is an estimate you control. Defaults are LA-typical starting points for a 1960s walk-up, not an appraisal: property tax at 1.25% of price growing at the Proposition 13 cap of 2%, management at 5% of effective gross income, reserves at $275 per unit per year. Loan program rates are indicative for September 2026 and move daily. The loan is sized at the lesser of your loan-to-value cap and the amount your year-one net operating income supports at the minimum coverage ratio — which is how a real lender will size it. Net operating income excludes debt service and capital expenditure by convention.
