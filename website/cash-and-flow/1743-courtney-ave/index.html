Most income properties in this part of town ask you to give something up. The units are cramped, the main house is an afterthought, or the location is a stretch. 1743 Courtney Ave doesn't make you choose. The front house is a fully remodeled three-bedroom Mediterranean that would work as a family home on its own. Behind it sit three separate guest houses: a 2-bed, a 1-bed and a studio.

The main house is about 2,600 square feet, with all three bedrooms upstairs and each one en suite. The ceilings are tall, the floors are wide-plank hardwood, and the kitchen opens to the yard through a Fleetwood pocket door. Out back there's a built-in BBQ and patio for entertaining. The whole lot is walled and gated, with an automatic gate on the driveway.

It's listed at $3,499,000, about $673 per square foot across all four structures. The seller can deliver it vacant. That matters more than it sounds, and we'll get to why.

Self-managed, NOI is about $161,000 (4.6% cap). Full lease-up at 25% down: NOI less $199,044 a year of principal and interest leaves about −$49,700 a year, or roughly −$4,100 a month.

A 4.3% cap and a 15× GRM are fair for the Hills, but the cap sits below the mortgage rate, so this does not cash-flow as a pure rental at any normal down payment. It isn't priced as one. It's priced like a family home with three units attached, and for an owner-user that's where the math gets interesting.

The house-hack play
Live in front.
Rent the back three.
$6,500
/ mo coming in

Live in the main house and lease the studio, the 1-bed and the 2-bed. The $6,500 a month they bring in covers close to 40% of principal and interest, bringing it down to about $10,100 a month, which is less than the $12,995 the front house alone would rent for. Property taxes (about $3,650 a month at this price) and insurance come on top. Even so, your housing cost lands well under what a comparable three-bedroom in Nichols Canyon would cost without any income behind it.

The units don't have to be rentals, either. They work as a guest house for visiting family, a live-in nanny or caregiver setup, a home office and studio, or a place for adult kids. You can lease one and keep two, then change the mix as your family does.

Why it works

  • ✓Delivered vacant. No tenants to inherit or buy out, and no existing rents locked in below market. You decide who lives where and what they pay.
  • ✓A real family house in front. Three bedrooms upstairs, each with its own bath, plus a powder room. The primary has a walk-in closet and a steam shower.
  • ✓Fully remodeled. Marble and stone counters, a walk-in pantry, central air and heat, a tankless water heater and double-pane windows.
  • ✓Built for entertaining. The kitchen opens to the yard through a Fleetwood pocket door, with a built-in BBQ outside.
  • ✓Private. A stucco perimeter wall, a gated entry, an automatic driveway gate and exterior security lighting.
  • ✓Four income streams, one lot. An 8,800 sq ft parcel with four separate dwellings gives you flexibility most single-family homes here can't.

Location

The listing places it in Nichols Canyon, in the Hollywood Hills West area, one of the most desirable residential pockets on this side of town. You're tucked into a quiet, tree-lined street that is still a short drive from:

  • —Runyon Canyon and the trail network along the hills.
  • —The Sunset Strip and West Hollywood dining.
  • —Laurel Canyon, for the quick route over to the Valley.
  • —Fairfax and Melrose shopping.

Homes here rarely come with income attached. Most buyers in this neighborhood are paying a similar price for a single house. Here you get the house and three units.

Lease all four for about $19.5K a month gross. It runs negative at 25% down today; the bet is land in the Hills with four dwellings on it.
⚠

Know before you go. Check the permits on the guest houses. The lot is zoned LAR1 and has three units behind the main house. Pull the permit history with LADBS and confirm each unit is legal (permitted guest house, ADU or otherwise) before you count on the rent. Rent control: depending on what's permitted and when, some units could fall under LA's Rent Stabilization Ordinance or state rules under AB 1482, which changes how rents and tenancies work once you lease. Verify square footage and condition: the figures here come from the listing and the seller, and the property is sold as-is. Inspect everything.

Quick answers

How much is 1743 Courtney Ave listed for?
$3,499,000, or about $673 per square foot across all four structures (5,198 sq ft total).
How many units are there?
Four: a 3-bed / 4-bath main house of about 2,600 sq ft, plus a 2-bed / 1-bath, a 1-bed / 1-bath and a studio at the rear.
What could it rent for?

Not as a pure rental at 25% down: our estimated 4.3% cap sits below the mortgage rate. As a house-hack it depends on your down payment, rate and how much of the property you occupy. Try it in the Cash & Flow calculator.
