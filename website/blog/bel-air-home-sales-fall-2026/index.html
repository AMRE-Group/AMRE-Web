What it means if you own in Bel Air.

Buyers have room to negotiate in Bel Air right now. 83% of recent sales closed under their asking price. That doesn't mean homes aren't selling: 21 did. It means the asking price is where talks start, and the homes that got full price were priced from real sales on day one.

The discount grows with price. Homes under $3.05M averaged 3% under asking, while homes $4.28M and up averaged 11% under asking. The top of the market has the most buyers sitting back.

The ones that beat asking had something in common: they launched at a believable number. 2100 Roscomare Rd sold 7% over asking at $2.13M. 10423 Oletha Ln sold 3% over asking at $1.23M.

Frequently asked questions.

How much are homes selling for in Bel Air right now?

Across 21 recent sales in Bel Air (90077), prices ran from $1.23M to $11.5M, with a median single-family sale of $3.7M. Source: MLS/public records via Compass, November 2026.

Are homes in Bel Air selling over asking?

Mostly not. 15 of 18 recent sales with a list price closed under asking and 3 closed over. By price band, homes under $3.05M averaged −3% against asking and homes $4.28M and up averaged −11%.
