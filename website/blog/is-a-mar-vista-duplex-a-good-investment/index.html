Is a Mar Vista duplex a good investment in 2026?

Three Mar Vista duplex sales in a 2026 sales-data list show published cap rates of 3.11% and 3.93% (the third is unpublished), so at 7.24% a 25%-down loan is not covered by rent. AMRE — the Compass Beverly Hills team led by Michael Abraham, licensed architect and REALTOR®, DRE# 02242095 — underwrites each before you offer.

What the numbers say

At the two sales with a published cap rate, net income at the printed cap covers roughly half to two-thirds of the mortgage payment on a 25%-down loan at 7.24% (DSCR about 0.51 and 0.64). Put plainly, these duplexes did not pay for themselves from rent at that rate. A higher down payment, a lower rate, higher rents or an owner living in one unit changes the picture, which is why we run each address rather than a Mar Vista average.

Two cautions on the sample: it is three sales, and the source does not date them. We would not read a neighborhood trend into it. For a broader look at how we underwrite small multifamily, see How to buy a duplex in Los Angeles and the investor calculator.

Rent rules in Mar Vista

Mar Vista sits in the City of Los Angeles. LAHD says a rental unit may be subject to the Rent Stabilization Ordinance if the property was built on or before October 1, 1978, and lists duplexes among covered unit types.4 The RSO’s allowable annual increase is 3% from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2027.5 A newer duplex is generally under California’s AB 1482 cap unless an exemption applies. Which one covers a given building changes the rent growth you can underwrite; our RSO vs AB 1482 guide has the 2026 caps and exemptions.

The ADU upside, by statute

The upside question for a duplex lot is how many units the state allows you to add. The table below summarizes California Government Code §66323 as published on the statute page we read.3 We did not assign rents to added units because we could not source them, and we did not assume any specific lot qualifies: zoning, lot configuration and permit history decide that.

What an architect checks first

Before we put numbers on a duplex we read the permit history, confirm the certificate of occupancy and the number of legal units, and look at where the buildings sit on the lot relative to the setbacks above. Unpermitted conversions and unclear unit counts change both the rent rules and the ADU math. Common Questions

Mar Vista duplexes, answered.

Is a Mar Vista duplex a good investment in 2026?

On the three sales we underwrote, the two with published cap rates sold at 3.11% and 3.93%. At the Freddie Mac 30-year average of 7.24% (October 29, 2026), a 25%-down loan on those sales produces a debt service coverage ratio of about 0.51 to 0.64, so rent does not cover the mortgage. The case for owning rests on appreciation, owner-occupancy or added units, not on day-one cash flow. This is general information, not lending or investment advice.

What cap rate do Mar Vista duplexes sell at?

In a 2026 sales-data list from Philippe Properties, Mar Vista (90066) duplex sales with a published cap rate were 3.11% (4626 Alla Rd, $830,000) and 3.93% (3734 Sawtelle Blvd, $1,050,000). The source does not publish sale dates, and two of the eight duplexes it lists give a cap rate, so treat this as a small sample.

Can I add ADUs to a Mar Vista duplex lot?

California Government Code §66323 says a local agency shall allow at least one ADU within an existing multifamily dwelling, up to 25 percent of existing units, and not more than eight detached ADUs on a lot with an existing multifamily dwelling, not exceeding the number of existing units. On a duplex lot that would be up to two detached ADUs, with rear and side setbacks of no more than four feet. Whether a specific lot qualifies depends on its zoning, site and permit history, which we check before you offer.

Which rent rules apply to a Mar Vista duplex?

Mar Vista is in the City of Los Angeles. LAHD says rental units may fall under the Rent Stabilization Ordinance if the property was built on or before October 1, 1978, and lists duplexes as covered. A newer duplex is generally under California's AB 1482 cap unless an exemption applies. Our RSO vs AB 1482 guide has the 2026 caps and exemptions.

What did AMRE leave out of this analysis?

Per-unit rents, ADU rents, sale dates, property taxes and insurance, and any DSCR-lender rate sheet. We could not source those figures, so we did not estimate them.
