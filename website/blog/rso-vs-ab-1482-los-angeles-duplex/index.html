RSO vs AB 1482: which rent rules cover your LA duplex?

It depends on the city and the build date. In the City of Los Angeles, a duplex built on or before October 1, 1978 is under the RSO (3% cap); most newer ones fall under state AB 1482 (8.7%) unless exempt. AMRE — the Compass Beverly Hills team led by Michael Abraham, licensed architect and REALTOR®, DRE# 02242095 — checks this before you offer.

By Michael Abraham · AMRE at Compass Updated September 24, 2026 6 min read
Updated September 24, 2026
General information, not legal advice. Rent rules change and turn on facts specific to each property. Confirm coverage with the city housing department and a California landlord-tenant attorney before you buy or raise rent.

Two duplexes on the same Westside street can run under completely different rent rules. The deciding facts are not the rent or the condition of the building — they are which city the parcel sits in, when its certificate of occupancy was issued, and whether the owner lives in one of the units. That is why we read the permit history before we underwrite a single rent number on a Cash & Flow deal.

The short version: a local rent-control ordinance, where one applies, sets the cap. Where no local ordinance applies, California’s Tenant Protection Act of 2019 (AB 1482) usually does, unless the building is less than 15 years old or another exemption fits.5 The table below walks every combination for the markets we work in.

City of Los Angeles: the October 1978 line

LAHD says a rental unit may be subject to the RSO if the property was built on or before October 1, 1978, and lists duplexes, two or more single-family dwellings on the same parcel, and ADUs among covered unit types. A single-family home that is the only residential structure on its parcel is exempt.1 The RSO’s allowable annual increase is 3% from July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2027.2 In late 2025 the City Council moved the formula to 90% of CPI with a 1% floor and a 4% ceiling, replacing the old 3%–8% band,2,4 and the extra percentage for landlord-paid utilities ended February 2, 2026.3

A post-1978 duplex in the City is outside the RSO but not unregulated. If its certificate of occupancy is more than 15 years old, AB 1482 caps increases, and the City’s Just Cause Ordinance separately governs evictions for most non-RSO units; it does not regulate rent.2 That is the situation on many blocks in West Los Angeles and West Adams, where pre-1978 and newer small buildings sit side by side.

AB 1482: the state floor

AB 1482 limits annual increases to 5% plus the regional change in the cost of living, or 10%, whichever is lower.5 For the Los Angeles area, CPI-U rose 3.7% in the 12 months ending April 2026,6 which puts the cap at 8.7% for increases taking effect from August 1, 2026.7 Exemptions that matter to duplex buyers: a certificate of occupancy issued within the previous 15 years; a duplex where the owner occupied one unit as a principal residence at the start of the tenancy and still does; and units already under a stricter local cap.5 The statute is scheduled to sunset January 1, 2030.5

Culver City and Santa Monica: their own ordinances

Culver City caps annual increases on rental units built on or before February 1, 1995, and exempts single-family homes, condominiums and townhomes; the cap is 3.25% for increases effective June 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. A separate Tenant Protections Ordinance requires for-cause or no-fault grounds for evictions across most rental units.8 The 1995 date mirrors the state Costa-Hawkins Act, which bars local caps on units with a certificate of occupancy issued after February 1, 1995.11

Santa Monica’s Charter controls rental units except new construction built after the Rent Control Charter Amendment was adopted, and exempts owner-occupied buildings of three units or fewer where a natural person owning at least 50% lives on the property; that exemption ends when the owner stops living there.10 The general adjustment for September 1, 2026 through August 31, 2027 is 2.6%, capped at $70 a month.9

Beach Cities: state law only

We found no local rent-control or rent-stabilization ordinance in Manhattan Beach, Hermosa Beach or Redondo Beach as of September 2026; published guides list all three as covered only by state law.12,13,14 A Beach Cities duplex more than 15 years old is therefore on the AB 1482 cap unless an exemption applies.

What we check before you offer

On every duplex we underwrite we pull the certificate of occupancy and permit history, confirm the jurisdiction line (it does not always follow the ZIP code), ask whether the units are registered with the city program, and model rent growth at the cap that actually applies — not a market assumption. Common Questions

RSO and AB 1482, answered.

Is my Los Angeles duplex covered by the RSO or by AB 1482?

In the City of Los Angeles, the Rent Stabilization Ordinance generally covers rental units, including duplexes, on properties built on or before October 1, 1978 (LAHD). A duplex with a later certificate of occupancy is generally outside the RSO, and falls under California’s AB 1482 rent cap if its certificate of occupancy is more than 15 years old and no other exemption applies (Civil Code §1947.12). Where a local ordinance with a stricter cap applies, AB 1482’s cap does not.

If I live in one unit of my duplex, am I exempt?

From AB 1482, often yes: the statute exempts a duplex where the owner occupied one unit as a principal residence at the start of the tenancy and continues to live there (Civil Code §1947.12). From the LA RSO, owner occupancy is not on LAHD’s published list of exemptions. In Santa Monica, the Charter exempts owner-occupied buildings of three units or fewer where a natural person owning at least 50% lives on the property; the exemption ends when the owner moves out.

How much can rent go up on an LA duplex in 2026?

It depends on which rule applies. LA RSO units: 3% for July 1, 2025 through June 30, 2027 (LAHD). AB 1482 units in the Los Angeles metro: 8.7% for increases from August 1, 2026 — 5% plus the 3.7% April 2026 Los Angeles-area CPI (BLS), capped at 10%. Culver City rent-controlled units: 3.25% for June 1, 2026 through June 30, 2027. Santa Monica controlled units: 2.6%, up to $70 a month, for September 1, 2026 through August 31, 2027.

Do Manhattan Beach, Hermosa Beach or Redondo Beach have rent control?

We found no local rent-control or rent-stabilization ordinance in any of the three as of September 2026; published owner and tenant guides list them as covered only by state law. That means AB 1482 applies to a duplex there if its certificate of occupancy is more than 15 years old and it is not otherwise exempt.

What about a newly built duplex or ADU?

AB 1482 exempts housing issued a certificate of occupancy within the previous 15 years, on a rolling basis. Separately, the state Costa-Hawkins Act (Civil Code §1954.52) bars local rent caps on units with a certificate of occupancy issued after February 1, 1995. Note that LAHD lists ADUs and junior ADUs among unit types that can be covered by the RSO on a qualifying property, so an ADU added behind a pre-1978 building should be checked, not assumed exempt.

How do I confirm the rules for a specific property before I buy?

Pull the certificate of occupancy and permit history, ask the city’s housing department whether the units are registered (LAHD for the City of Los Angeles, the Culver City and Santa Monica rent-control programs in those cities), and have a California landlord-tenant attorney review anything unclear. We check jurisdiction, build date and permit history on every duplex we underwrite.
