Living in Brentwood, LA: Is It Worth Moving To?

Quiet estate streets, top-tier schools, and a five-minute drive to Santa Monica — an honest look at what it costs to live in Brentwood in 2026, who's actually moving here, and what changes if you're coming from out of state.

Michael Abraham
Principal Agent, Architect · Sep 28, 2026 · 6 min read

If you're weighing living in Brentwood, Los Angeles — especially moving here from out of state — the short answer is that it's one of the Westside's quietest, most private addresses: wide estate streets, top-tier schools, and a five-minute drive to Santa Monica without the beach-town density.

Is Brentwood a good place to live?

For buyers who want privacy and space without leaving the Westside, yes. Brentwood sits between Santa Monica and Bel Air, climbing from San Vicente Boulevard up into Mandeville Canyon and the hills below Mulholland. It's known for low density, mature trees, and some of LA's most sought-after public and private schools — which is why it consistently draws executives, entertainment-industry families, and out-of-state buyers relocating for privacy and school quality rather than nightlife or walkability.

Is it expensive?

Brentwood is one of the priciest Westside addresses. The median sale price is about $2.42 million (roughly $985 per square foot) — down close to 10% year-over-year even as price per square foot rose about 8%, a sign the mix of what's selling has shifted toward smaller, more efficient homes. Homes are taking about 56 days to sell, roughly three weeks faster than a year ago.

Who moves here.

Brentwood attracts families prioritizing schools (Brentwood School, Paul Revere Charter, and top private options nearby), entertainment and business executives who want privacy close to the studios and Westside offices, and downsizers moving from larger estates elsewhere in the country who still want space and quiet. Out-of-state relocators — especially from the Bay Area and New York — often choose Brentwood specifically because it trades some walkability for calm, established, low-turnover streets.

Lifestyle & architecture.

San Vicente Boulevard's median jogging path and the Brentwood Country Mart anchor daily life, with the Getty Center and Mandeville Canyon hiking trails minutes away. Architecturally, Brentwood holds some of LA's best mid-century modern housing stock alongside traditional estates and newer construction — the kind of range where an architect's read on quality and provenance genuinely changes what a home is worth paying for.

If you're moving from out of state.

Buyers relocating to Brentwood most often ask about schools (confirm the specific assignment before you buy — boundaries matter block to block), commute (the 405 and San Vicente give quick Westside and Valley access), and pricing (the ~$2.42M median is skewing toward smaller homes right now, so more entry points exist than the headline number suggests).

Living here at a glance

  • One of the Westside's quietest, most private addresses — estate streets and canyon access.
  • ~$2.42M median, but price/sq ft rose ~8% as smaller homes sell more often.
  • Top-tier schools drive much of the demand; confirm boundaries block by block.
  • Currently more buyer-friendly than in recent years — homes selling faster, prices softening.

Is Brentwood a good place to live?

Yes, for buyers prioritizing privacy, space, and schools. Brentwood offers quiet estate streets, canyon hiking access, and some of LA's top-rated schools, with a quick drive to Santa Monica and the Westside job centers.

Is Brentwood safe?

Brentwood is generally considered one of the lower-density, lower-crime Westside neighborhoods, which is part of its appeal to families and executives relocating from out of state.

What are home prices in Brentwood?

The median sale price is about $2.42 million, or roughly $985 per square foot, as of 2026 — down close to 10% year-over-year, though price per square foot rose about 8%, reflecting a shift toward smaller homes selling.

What should someone moving to Brentwood from out of state know?

Confirm the exact school assignment before buying since boundaries shift block to block, budget realistically against the ~$2.42M median, and expect a quieter, more private pace of life than Santa Monica or Venice.

Is Brentwood a buyer's or seller's market in 2026?

Prices are down nearly 10% year-over-year and homes are selling about three weeks faster than last year — conditions that favor buyers more than they have in several years, especially on larger, higher-priced listings.
