What it means if you own in Hancock Park and Mid-Wilshire.

Buyers have room to negotiate in Hancock Park and Mid-Wilshire right now. 89% of recent sales closed under their asking price. That doesn't mean homes aren't selling: 147 did. It means the asking price is where talks start, and the homes that got full price were priced from real sales on day one.

The discount is similar at every price point, about 5% under asking on average. Pricing, not price band, separated the fast sales from the slow ones.

The ones that beat asking had something in common: they launched at a believable number. 436 N Genesee Ave sold 22% over asking at $1.7M. 253 S St Andrews Pl sold 17% over asking at $1.75M.

Condition and layout move the number more than size. On Warner Dr, two homes within a few hundred square feet of each other sold $458K apart ($2.2M and $2.66M). Updated, move-in-ready homes close near asking; homes that need work absorb the discount.

Small buildings are trading. 27 duplexes to small apartment buildings sold here, at a median of $1.95M. For owners, the question is often keep-or-sell, which is a rent-versus-net calculation we can run for you.

Frequently asked questions.

How much are homes selling for in Hancock Park and Mid-Wilshire right now?

Across 147 recent sales in Hancock Park and Mid-Wilshire (90004, 90005, 90010, 90020, 90036, 90048), prices ran from $540K to $7.03M, with a median single-family sale of $2.13M and a median condo of $840K. Source: MLS/public records via Compass, November 2026.

Are homes in Hancock Park and Mid-Wilshire selling over asking?

Mostly not. 95 of 107 recent sales with a list price closed under asking and 12 closed over. By price band, homes under $1.88M averaged −3% against asking and homes $2.66M and up averaged −5%.
