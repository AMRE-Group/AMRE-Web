What it means if you own in Mid-City and West Adams.

Buyers have room to negotiate in Mid-City and West Adams right now. 74% of recent sales closed under their asking price. That doesn't mean homes aren't selling: 210 did. It means the asking price is where talks start, and the homes that got full price were priced from real sales on day one.

Entry-level homes are softer than the top. Homes under $865K averaged 5% under asking, while homes $1.18M and up held closer to asking (−2%).

The ones that beat asking had something in common: they launched at a believable number. 1705 S Genesee Ave sold 18% over asking at $1.6M. 5443 Blackwelder St sold 12% over asking at $890K.

Condition and layout move the number more than size. On S Genesee Ave, two homes within a few hundred square feet of each other sold $422K apart ($1.18M and $1.6M). Updated, move-in-ready homes close near asking; homes that need work absorb the discount.

Small buildings are trading. 66 duplexes to small apartment buildings sold here, at a median of $1.21M. For owners, the question is often keep-or-sell, which is a rent-versus-net calculation we can run for you.
