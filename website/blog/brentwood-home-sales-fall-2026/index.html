What it means if you own in Brentwood.

Buyers have room to negotiate in Brentwood right now. 90% of recent sales closed under their asking price. That doesn't mean homes aren't selling: 84 did. It means the asking price is where talks start, and the homes that got full price were priced from real sales on day one.

The discount grows with price. Homes under $2.98M averaged 7% under asking, while homes $5.15M and up averaged 9% under asking. The top of the market has the most buyers sitting back.

The ones that beat asking had something in common: they launched at a believable number. 11628 Montana Ave sold 19% over asking at $1.41M. 339 S Carmelina Ave sold 9% over asking at $11.26M.

Condition and layout move the number more than size. On S Barrington Ave, two homes within a few hundred square feet of each other sold $710K apart ($2.05M and $2.76M). Updated, move-in-ready homes close near asking; homes that need work absorb the discount.

Frequently asked questions.

How much are homes selling for in Brentwood right now?

Across 84 recent sales in Brentwood (90049), prices ran from $970K to $38.65M, with a median single-family sale of $3.6M and a median condo of $1.41M. Source: MLS/public records via Compass, November 2026.

Are homes in Brentwood selling over asking?

Mostly not. 52 of 58 recent sales with a list price closed under asking and 6 closed over. By price band, homes under $2.98M averaged −7% against asking and homes $5.15M and up averaged −9%.
