What it means if you own in Santa Monica, Venice and Marina del Rey.

Buyers have room to negotiate in Santa Monica, Venice and Marina del Rey right now. 73% of recent sales closed under their asking price. That doesn't mean homes aren't selling: 394 did. It means the asking price is where talks start, and the homes that got full price were priced from real sales on day one.

The discount is similar at every price point, about 2% under asking on average. Pricing, not price band, separated the fast sales from the slow ones.

The ones that beat asking had something in common: they launched at a believable number. 827 Yale St sold 23% over asking at $3.92M. 2519 31st St sold 23% over asking at $2.45M.

Condition and layout move the number more than size. On Marguerita Ave, two homes within a few hundred square feet of each other sold $4.25M apart ($8M and $12.25M). Updated, move-in-ready homes close near asking; homes that need work absorb the discount.

Small buildings are trading. 48 duplexes to small apartment buildings sold here, at a median of $2.08M. For owners, the question is often keep-or-sell, which is a rent-versus-net calculation we can run for you.
