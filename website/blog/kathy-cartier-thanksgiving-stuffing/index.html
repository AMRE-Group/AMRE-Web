Kathy Cartier’s Thanksgiving stuffing

A family secret, passed down from grandma. In Ania’s experience there is a 0% chance it won’t get compliments, and a very good chance you’ll be asked to make it again next year.

From Ania De Pourbaix · AMRE at CompassOne 9×13 dish350°F · 35–45 min

Every family has one dish that isn’t up for debate. In ours, it’s this one. Ania and Scott are now the official “stuffing makers” for family and friends, and this is the recipe they make. It’s simple, it’s buttery, and the apple is Ania’s own touch.

Batch note: makes one standard 9×13-inch dish. Double it if you’re stuffing the turkey and still want a side pan.

Ingredients

The base & vegetables
2 boxes (12 oz each)Cubed stuffing / dressing mix
2 sticksButter, for sautéing
3 cupsDiced celery, leaves included
2 largeOnions, diced
2 cupsChopped parsley
1Granny Smith apple, peeled and diced (optional, Ania’s addition)
Seasonings
1½ teaspoonsDried sage
1½ teaspoonsPoultry seasoning
½ teaspoonSalt
¼ teaspoonBlack pepper
Liquid & binder
1 stickButter, for melting
2 cupsWater
2 largeEggs, beaten

Method

  1. Preheat. Oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9×13-inch baking dish.
  2. Sauté the vegetables. Melt 2 sticks of butter in a large skillet over medium heat. Add the celery, onions and parsley and cook until softened. Set aside.
  3. Combine. In a large bowl, toss both boxes of dressing cubes with the warm vegetables. Add the apple if you’re using it.
  4. Season. Add the sage, poultry seasoning, salt and pepper, and toss to spread it evenly.
  5. Warm the liquid. In a small saucepan, melt the last stick of butter into the 2 cups of water. Set aside.
  6. Bind. Pour the beaten eggs over the mixture and toss to coat.
  7. Moisten. Pour the warm butter-water over slowly, tossing gently, until the cubes are moist but not soggy. The eggs add moisture too, so go easy. Taste and add seasoning if needed.
  8. Bake. Transfer to the dish, cover tightly with foil and bake at 350°F for 35 to 45 minutes, checking near the end so it doesn’t over-brown. Tip: pull the foil for the last 5–10 minutes for a crisper top.
