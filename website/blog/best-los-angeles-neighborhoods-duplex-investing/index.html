The best LA neighborhoods for a duplex in 2026

There is no single best neighborhood for a duplex. There is a best one for your plan: cash flow, a house-hack, or long-term appreciation. Here are eight places we look, with the sold prices, rents and rent rules we could source. The short answer
  • For a house-hack: Mid-City and Arlington Heights (90019) have the most duplex sales, with 29 closings from $750K to $2.4M in the year to April 2026.
  • For the lowest entry: West Adams (90016 duplexes $700K–$1.125M) and the blocks near USC, where we underwrote a $949K vacant duplex.
  • For appreciation: Mar Vista. Caps of about 3.1%–3.9% mean rent won’t cover a 25%-down loan, but you’re on the Westside with ADU upside.
  • Outside LA City rules: Inglewood has its own rent ordinance; Long Beach generally follows state AB 1482.
  • Financing: FHA lets an owner-occupant buy a 2–4 unit home with 3.5% down; the 2026 LA County limit for a duplex is $1,599,375.

“Where should I buy my first duplex?” is the question we hear most at Cash & Flow. The honest answer starts with another question: what is the duplex for? If you plan to live in one unit and rent the other, the neighborhood has to work for your life first. If you are buying for rent, the numbers come first, and in most of Los Angeles at a 7.18% mortgage rate12 they are tight. If you are buying for the long run, land, location and what you can add to the lot matter more than this year’s cap rate.

Below are eight areas we work in, with only the evidence we could source and cite. Where nobody publishes duplex sales data, we say so rather than guess.

How to read the table

Three things decide whether a duplex works. Price per door: a $950K duplex is $475K a unit, the cost of a one-bedroom condo on the Westside. Rent per unit: 2BR averages run from about $2,350 in Long Beach to about $3,000 in Mar Vista.79 Rent rules: nearly every older duplex in the City of Los Angeles is under the Rent Stabilization Ordinance, which allows 3% a year from July 2025 through June 2027 and expressly covers duplexes.13 A newer building generally falls under the state AB 1482 cap of 8.7% for increases from August 2026.15 A vacant duplex lets you set the starting rent; an occupied one comes with the rents already in place.

Cash flow: where it gets close

Countywide, Kidder Mathews put the average Los Angeles multifamily cap rate at 5.9% in Q3 2026,4 but that average is driven by larger buildings. On duplexes, the published caps we found run from about 3.1% in Mar Vista to the mid-5s in Mid-City, and fourplexes in 90016 reached 5.74%–7.28%.123 If day-one cash flow is the goal, look at West Adams, the blocks around USC and, for larger buildings, triplexes and fourplexes rather than duplexes. House-hacking: where it fits best

For owner-occupants, FHA financing allows a 2–4 unit purchase with 3.5% down at a 580+ credit score,16 and the 2026 FHA limit for a two-unit property in Los Angeles County is $1,599,375.17 That puts most of Mid-City, West Adams, Leimert Park and Highland Park within reach. The tenant’s rent offsets your payment, and in the City of LA, living in one unit can matter for which rent rules apply. Our Culver City vs Leimert Park breakdown compares two house-hacks side by side.

Appreciation and upside: the Westside play

Mar Vista duplexes rarely cash flow. Our Mar Vista underwriting found debt coverage ratios of about 0.51 and 0.64 at 25% down. Buyers pay for the location and the lot. State law allows ADUs on lots with an existing multifamily building, and on a duplex lot that can mean up to two detached units. That is where the long-term return comes from. SB 79, the state transit-housing law that took effect July 1, 2026, gets a lot of attention, but the City of Los Angeles has adopted ordinances that pause it on nearly all city sites until about 2030, pending state review.18 We would not pay for SB 79 upside inside the city today.

Common Questions

LA duplex investing, answered.

What is the best neighborhood to buy a duplex in Los Angeles?

It depends on the plan. For house-hacking, Mid-City and Arlington Heights (90019) have the deepest duplex market, with 29 sales from $750K to $2.4M in the year to April 2026. For a lower entry price, West Adams (90016 duplexes $700K-$1.125M) and the blocks near USC. For long-term appreciation and ADU upside, Mar Vista, though rent rarely covers the mortgage there.

How much does a duplex cost in Los Angeles in 2026?

Published closed sales in the year to April 2026 ranged from $700K to $1.125M in 90016 (West Adams), $750K to $2.4M in 90019 (Mid-City), and $830K to $1.75M in Mar Vista (90066), per Philippe Properties. Price depends heavily on condition, vacancy, lot size and whether the building is under rent control.

Do Los Angeles duplexes cash flow?

Often not on day one at 25% down. Published duplex cap rates we found ranged from about 3.1% in Mar Vista to the mid-5% range in Mid-City, and the 30-year mortgage rate averaged 7.18% the week of November 5, 2026. Cash flow improves with a larger down payment, a vacant building, higher-rent units or a triplex or fourplex.

Can I buy a duplex in Los Angeles with an FHA loan?

Yes, if you live in one of the units. FHA allows owner-occupants to buy 2-4 unit properties with 3.5% down at a 580 or higher credit score. The 2026 FHA loan limit for a two-unit property in Los Angeles County is $1,599,375. Confirm with a lender.

Are Los Angeles duplexes rent controlled?

Most older ones are. In the City of Los Angeles, the Rent Stabilization Ordinance generally covers rental properties first built on or before October 1, 1978, and duplexes are covered; the allowable increase is 3% from July 2025 to June 2027. Newer buildings generally fall under the state AB 1482 cap. Inglewood and Santa Monica have their own rules.
