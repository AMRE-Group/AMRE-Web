What it means if you own in Studio City and Sherman Oaks.

Buyers have room to negotiate in Studio City and Sherman Oaks right now. 80% of recent sales closed under their asking price. That doesn't mean homes aren't selling: 244 did. It means the asking price is where talks start, and the homes that got full price were priced from real sales on day one.

The discount is similar at every price point, about 5% under asking on average. Pricing, not price band, separated the fast sales from the slow ones.

The ones that beat asking had something in common: they launched at a believable number. 5426 Radford Ave sold 20% over asking at $1.38M. 3267 Laurel Canyon Blvd sold 15% over asking at $2.35M.

Condition and layout move the number more than size. On Otsego St, two homes within a few hundred square feet of each other sold $875K apart ($1.77M and $2.65M). Updated, move-in-ready homes close near asking; homes that need work absorb the discount.

Small buildings are trading. 6 duplexes to small apartment buildings sold here, at a median of $2.06M. For owners, the question is often keep-or-sell, which is a rent-versus-net calculation we can run for you.

How much are homes selling for in Studio City and Sherman Oaks right now?

Across 244 recent sales in Studio City and Sherman Oaks (91604, 91602, 91423, 91403, 91607), prices ran from $482K to $10.25M, with a median single-family sale of $1.77M and a median condo of $758K. Source: MLS/public records via Compass, November 2026.

Mostly not. 144 of 181 recent sales with a list price closed under asking and 36 closed over. By price band, homes under $1.46M averaged −4% against asking and homes $2.16M and up averaged −6%.
