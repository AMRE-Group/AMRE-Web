What it means if you own in Baldwin Hills and Baldwin Vista.

Buyers have room to negotiate in Baldwin Hills and Baldwin Vista right now. 71% of recent sales closed under their asking price. That doesn't mean homes aren't selling: 122 did. It means the asking price is where talks start, and the homes that got full price were priced from real sales on day one.

The discount is similar at every price point, about 3% under asking on average. Pricing, not price band, separated the fast sales from the slow ones.

The ones that beat asking had something in common: they launched at a believable number. 4917 Orinda Ave sold 17% over asking at $1.4M. 3857 Welland Ave sold 13% over asking at $1.52M.

Condition and layout move the number more than size. On S Norton Ave, two homes within a few hundred square feet of each other sold $459K apart ($810K and $1.27M). Updated, move-in-ready homes close near asking; homes that need work absorb the discount.

Small buildings are trading. 16 duplexes to small apartment buildings sold here, at a median of $1.21M. For owners, the question is often keep-or-sell, which is a rent-versus-net calculation we can run for you.
