Beach Cities · South Bay

El Segundo
real estate agent.

A small town between LAX and the ocean — its own school district, a real Main Street, and mid-century homes on a walkable grid a short ride from the beach.

ZIP 90245

Last updated: September 22, 2026 · By Michael Abraham, licensed architect & REALTOR®, DRE# 02242095

El Segundo is the Beach City people choose for the school district and the commute. The median sale price was about $1.70M in the three months to June 2026 (Redfin), on only 28 sales, with homes going under contract in a median 33 days.

It is small and self-contained: El Segundo Unified serves the whole city, Main Street is a genuine downtown, and the aerospace and tech employers on the east side are minutes away. The trade-offs are LAX to the north and the refinery to the south, and they affect some blocks far more than others.

At a glance

El Segundo — five things to know.

01

One-city school district

El Segundo Unified serves only El Segundo. For many buyers that is the whole decision.

02

Main Street

A walkable downtown of restaurants and shops sits in the middle of the residential grid, which is rare in the South Bay.

03

Employers next door

Aerospace, defense and technology offices line the east side of the city, which makes El Segundo one of the shortest commutes in the region for people who work there.

04

Airport and refinery

LAX borders the north and the Chevron refinery much of the south. Noise and views vary block by block, so visit at different times of day.

El Segundo FAQ

El Segundo, explained.

How much does a home cost in El Segundo?

Redfin put the El Segundo median sale price at about $1.70M for the three months to June 2026, down 9.7% year over year on a small sample of 28 sales, at roughly $1,190 per square foot. With so few sales, one or two large homes can swing the median, so look at the comps for your specific block.

What school district serves El Segundo?

El Segundo Unified School District, a small single-city district that includes El Segundo High School. Its size is one of the main reasons families choose the city.

Is El Segundo a good place to live if I work nearby?

It is one of the shortest commutes in the South Bay for anyone working at LAX or at the aerospace and technology employers along the city's east side, and the Metro C Line runs along that edge. Downtown along Main Street keeps the residential grid feeling like a small town.

What about noise from LAX and the refinery?

El Segundo sits directly south of LAX and next to the Chevron refinery, which occupies much of the city's south side. Flight paths and prevailing winds affect blocks differently, so visit at several times of day and check the airport noise contours for the specific address before you buy.

Does El Segundo have rent control?

There is no city rent-control ordinance. Qualifying rentals fall under California's statewide Tenant Protection Act (AB 1482). Confirm the status of a specific property with an attorney.
